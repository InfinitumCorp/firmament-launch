What it does

One wallet.
Three networks.

Most people juggle a different wallet for each chain. $FIRM Wallet™ holds Solana, Ethereum and Base side by side, so every Infinitum token sits in one balance, under one set of keys.

Custody

Self-custody

Your keys are created and kept on your device. Infinitum never holds them and can't move your funds.

Networks

Solana, Ethereum, Base

One address book, one portfolio and one history across all three networks.

Exchange

Swap for any token

Swap $FIRM™ or any Infinitum token for any token on Solana, Ethereum or Base, without leaving the app. Not just ours.

03
